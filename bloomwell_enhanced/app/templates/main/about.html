About BloomWell

Built by women, for women — a safe, inclusive platform connecting women with fitness and wellbeing communities across the West Midlands.

Our Mission 💜

BloomWell was created to bridge the gap women face when trying to access safe, inclusive, and reliable fitness and wellbeing opportunities. Many women struggle to find suitable activities due to limited information, safety concerns, and a lack of inclusivity in mixed-gender environments.

We believe every woman deserves access to a space where she can move, grow, and connect — free from judgement, built on trust, and tailored to her needs. From yoga to HIIT, walking groups to mindfulness workshops, BloomWell makes it easy to find, book and join women-only spaces near you.

🛡️

Safety First

All providers are verified by our admin team before listing. Women-only spaces are clearly marked and monitored.

🌍

Inclusive Always

Multilingual support, cultural sensitivity, and accessibility features built into every part of the platform.

🤝

Community Driven

A moderated forum where women share tips, stories, and encouragement in a safe, supportive environment.

⭐

Verified Reviews

Only women who have attended classes can leave reviews, so you can trust every rating on our platform.

📍

Local Focus

Rooted in Redditch, Bromsgrove and Worcester, with plans to expand across the West Midlands and beyond.

🌱

Holistic Wellness

From physical fitness to mental health, nutrition to mindfulness — we support the whole woman.

Academic Context

BloomWell is developed as part of the ACCA6020 Independent Project module. The platform demonstrates how technology can be used to empower women through accessible, inclusive digital services. Development followed Agile methodology across five structured sprints, from requirements gathering and system design through to testing, deployment and evaluation.
